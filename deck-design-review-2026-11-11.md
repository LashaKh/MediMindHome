## Part 1. Horacio's design rules (with timestamps)

| # | Rule | What he said, in short | Time |
|---|------|------------------------|------|
| 1 | **Ideas, not slides** | Software and vision are hard to picture. Make the idea visible so it feels concrete. | 07:46–09:17 |
| 2 | **The deck is your brand** | Slides are a brand touchpoint, not an afterthought. Investors can tell who treats design as a value. | 09:17–13:15 |
| 3 | **Three parts** | Message (script) + visual story (deck) + delivery. This lecture is the visual story. | 11:13 |
| 4 | **Pick 2–3 attribute words first** | e.g. "bold, iconic, immersive". They decide font size, how simple, how many images. | 13:15–15:16 |
| 5 | **Placement creates meaning** | Close = related. First = more important. | 15:16 |
| 6 | **Five tools: contrast, flow, hierarchy, proximity, white space** | Use them on every slide. | 15:16–16:52 |
| 7 | **Contrast** | Make the one key thing pop with size, colour or shape. Elegant, not "all the bells and whistles". | 16:52–18:51 |
| 8 | **Flow** | People must know the reading order: left to right, big to small, arrows. A confused audience decodes instead of listening. | 18:51–20:46 |
| 9 | **Hierarchy** | Big bold title first, smaller text after. Clear 1st, 2nd, 3rd. | 20:46–22:45 |
| 10 | **Proximity** | Things that belong together sit together. An idea that does not fit belongs on another slide. | 22:45–24:27 |
| 11 | **White space** | Founders fear it. Don't throw everything on a slide. One big data point + your voice beats paragraphs. | 24:27–26:13 |
| 12 | **Rhythm** | Alternate heavier and lighter slides. Simple slides let people follow at your pace. "If they get lost… you're going to lose them forever." | 26:13–27:54 |
| 13 | **Spoken deck ≠ emailed deck** | A slide you talk over shows only the highlight. Full data lives in a handout, email or your voice. | 27:54–29:36 |
| 14 | **Layered cake for data** | Background → data → one highlight. "Simplicity matters more than data." Minimal looks more pro, not less. | 29:36–31:25 |
| 15 | **One consistent system** | Lock palette, fonts, photo style, icons. Switching styles confuses people. | 31:25–33:18 |
| 16 | **Backgrounds as a rhythm set** | e.g. black, white, grey + one "poppy" colour background. Neutral holds complex ideas; the colourful one holds a simple, powerful idea. | 33:18–36:44 |
| 17 | **Use a grid** | Titles and text land on the same lines. Off-grid elements make people notice mistakes. | 34:52–36:44 |
| 18 | **Colour sets mood and category** | Health, banking and education each have a colour mood. Keep your brand colours, allow an extra accent when needed. | 36:44–40:32 |
| 19 | **Readable fonts, one big family** | An investor once had to ask a founder to read a slide aloud. Use one font family with many weights (his example: Gilroy). Simple is best. | 40:32–45:38 |
| 20 | **One image system** | Photos must look like one family (same edit, same style), whether stock or AI. Same for illustrations. | 45:38–47:36 |
| 21 | **Style your diagrams** | Preset charts look "too standard". Custom-styled diagrams are more memorable. | 47:36–49:13 |
| 22 | **One icon family** | Mixed icon sets look careless. | 47:36–49:13 |
| 23 | **Process: rough draft → wireframe → final** | Close the script first, then rough-draft fast. Bring a rough draft to the 1:1s. | 49:13–55:46 |

Related rule from Dina's storytelling session (same day, sibling pack): the demo-day deck only reinforces a few things you say, and it must match your words (Dina 09:19).

## Part 3. Slide by slide

### 00 · Cover
*Script:* "In four years, practicing medicine without an AI will be malpractice. Hello, I'm Lasha, a cardiologist…"

- **Follows the rules:** Lots of white space, one idea, strong contrast (white on brand blue). Says "operating system". Good brand moment (rule 2).
- **Breaks:** Nothing big. Nobody sees who is talking.
- **Upgrade:** Keep it. Optional: a small line under the tagline, "Dr. Lasha Khoshtaria · Cardiologist, co-founder". Do **not** put the malpractice line on the slide; your voice carries it and the cover stays clean.

### 01 · Validation
*Script:* 15 months → signed contract with a 25-hospital network → second large hospital agreed to replace all its software → ~2M patient encounters.

- **Follows:** Big "25" is a strong single data point (rules 11, 14). Clear left-to-right flow. Real logo.
- **Breaks:** Wrong facts vs the script (13 vs 15 months; "live across all 25" vs "signed"). Four numbers compete (25, Live, 1,000+, <1 month), so there is no single highlight (rule 14). The two numbers you actually say (second hospital, ~2M encounters) are missing. Rule 13: what you don't say should not be on screen.
- **Upgrade:**
  - Headline: **"15 months: from idea to a 25-hospital network."**
  - Left: keep the big **25 hospitals · SIGNED** card.
  - Right: replace the 3 stats with **two** items only: "**+1** large hospital: full software replacement" and "**~2M** patient encounters".
  - Cut "Live · Labs & Imaging", "1,000+ doctors", "<1 month rollout" (move to appendix/Q&A).

### 02 · The problem is the whole legacy system (script: Growth / All-in)
*Script:* "I used to think an AI copilot would fix healthcare. I was wrong. The problem is the whole old system, so we're replacing all of it."

- **Follows:** The best slide for Horacio's rules: one line, huge white space, clear hierarchy (small line → big line), contrast on "whole legacy system" (rules 11, 12, 16).
- **Breaks:** Light-on-light, so it looks like the slides around it. No rhythm change (rule 16).
- **Upgrade:** Make this one of two "punch" slides: full brand-blue background (same calm navy→blue gradient as the cover, not neon), white text. Optional third line to match your words: "**So we're replacing all of it.**"

### 03 · "We are doctors building for doctors" (not in the script)
- **Follows:** Real photo from a clinic (authentic, rule 20). Clean list.
- **Breaks:** The script has no words for it. It adds a slide you must click through silently, and it repeats slide 07 ("replaces every software", "AI-native OS"). "One codebase" is a builder word investors don't need.
- **Upgrade:** **Cut it** from the main flow. Reuse the clinic photo on the Case slide (04), where it supports "a case from my practice".

### 04 · Case study (script: Case → Problem)
*Script:* 2 a.m., stomach pain after a heart procedure, focused on the heart, missed the pancreas, 20 days ICU. "In emergency rooms, about 1 in 18 patients gets the wrong diagnosis. At 2 a.m., people miss things. MediMind doesn't."

- **Follows:** Good left-to-right timeline (rule 8). Red for the bad path is clear contrast. Strong headline that is also a script line.
- **Breaks:**
  - The "**1 in 18**" number, your biggest problem proof, is not on screen.
  - The "WITH MEDIMIND" row gives the answer here, but the script saves the answer for "What We Are" ("Picture that 2 a.m. case with MediMind running"). Showing it twice weakens the payoff.
  - Label "REAL CASE · MEDIMIND PILOT HOSPITAL" can read as "MediMind was used in this case". The script says "a case from my practice".
  - "02:00" appears twice (headline and big clock). A small empty box floats under "Eyes on the heart" (visual bug, rule 17).
- **Upgrade:**
  - Top half: the "WITHOUT" timeline only: *Eyes on the heart → Hours lost → Pancreatitis found → 20 days in ICU*.
  - Bottom half (second click): one giant number, **"1 in 18"** ER patients get the wrong diagnosis, tiny source "AHRQ, 2022".
  - Label: "REAL CASE · FROM MY PRACTICE". Remove the "WITH MEDIMIND" row and the empty box.
  - Keep the headline as the last line you say.

### 05 · Team
*Script:* one sentence, about Irakli: CEO, 9 years at Riot Games, 15-person studio → 500.

- **Follows:** Real photos, consistent card style (rule 15). Founders above advisors (hierarchy).
- **Breaks:**
  - About 6 seconds of speech, but 6 faces and ~20 small tags (rule 11).
  - Irakli's title and proof don't match: slide says "Product & Strategy" and "Riot Games · PM"; script says CEO and 15 → 500.
  - "The perfect team for the job" is a claim, not a proof.
  - Visual bug: the "Healthycore · Pilot" chips show a broken icon over the "H".
  - Founder photos are different sizes (Lasha's is bigger than Irakli's).
- **Upgrade:**
  - Headline: **"Doctors, plus a team that has scaled before."**
  - Two big founder cards, same photo size, one proof each:
    - Irakli Pkhovelishvili · **Co-founder & CEO** · "9 years at Riot Games · 15 → 500 people"
    - Dr. Lasha Khoshtaria · **Co-founder · Cardiologist** · one line of proof (e.g. "Built the 25-hospital deal")
  - Advisors: a thin row of 4 small faces with name + one word, or move to the appendix. Fix the chip icon.

### 06 · Competition
*Script:* "Our competitors are AI tools, AI add-ons, and old medical record systems. None of them runs a hospital proactively. MediMind does. We replace all three with one system and an agentic AI harness…, making us a category-defining company."

- **Follows:** White space; three equal cards; one icon family; "category-defining company" is there.
- **Breaks:**
  - MediMind is not on the slide. There is no contrast element, only three grey boxes (rule 7). The key idea "None of them runs a hospital proactively. MediMind does." is invisible.
  - Words differ from the script: "AI overlays" vs "AI add-ons"; "Legacy EHRs" vs "old medical record systems". "Universal codebase" is a builder word.
  - Headline is two lines and gives the conclusion before the comparison (flow).
- **Upgrade:**
  - Headline: **"None of them runs a hospital proactively."**
  - Row of three grey cards: *AI tools · AI add-ons · Old record systems*, each with a small grey ✗ "not proactive".
  - Arrow down to one bright, wide MediMind card (brand blue): "**One system + agentic AI harness. Runs the hospital proactively.** ✓"
  - Footer line: "…making us a **category-defining company**."

### 07 · Inside MediMind (script: What We Are)
*Script:* "An AI that can run the whole hospital and make medical decisions within limits each hospital sets. Picture that 2 a.m. case… spots the pancreatitis, orders the blood tests, calls the specialists, arranges the scans, books an ICU bed, and handles the billing. One patient walks in. The whole hospital moves."

- **Follows:** A real custom diagram, not a preset (rule 21). Hub-and-spoke shows "one system". The 2 a.m. strip ties back to the case. "The whole hospital moves." is there.
- **Breaks:**
  - The densest slide: 12 module cards with jargon (ESI, EWS, I-PASS, Cornerstone3D, Beers, DDI, FHIR R4) at small size. Nobody can read it while you talk (rules 11, 13, 19).
  - The strip has 7 steps (incl. "Fluids started", "Surgery paged") but you say 6 different ones (spots, blood tests, specialists, scans, ICU bed, billing).
  - "One patient walks in." is missing. The must-keep line should be the headline.
  - "Within limits each hospital sets" is not on screen. For a slide that says AI makes medical decisions, the guardrail must be visible.
  - The headline "MediMind is… all in one, AI-native hospital-wide OS" repeats the cover and is two lines.
- **Upgrade:**
  - Headline: **"One patient walks in. The whole hospital moves."**
  - Cards show **names only** (ER, Lab, Imaging, Specialists, ICU, Billing…). Delete every sub-line.
  - Animate the script: as you say each step, its card lights up and a line draws from the hub: Spots pancreatitis → Blood tests → Specialists → Scans → ICU bed → Billing. Same 6 steps, same order, in the bottom strip.
  - Small tag near the hub: "**Within limits each hospital sets.**"
  - Optional: make this the second "punch" background (dark navy) so the payoff stands out.

### 08 · Georgia
*Script:* "We chose Georgia on purpose. It's a world hub for first-in-human trials, so what we prove here counts in the West, years sooner. My hospital is our lab."

- **Follows:** A real map in the brand style (rule 20). Clear left/right split. The highlight box uses contrast well.
- **Breaks:**
  - "25 live hospitals" conflicts with "signed" in the script. The map shows about 10 pins, not 25.
  - "16–28 months of runway…" is a number you don't say (rule 13).
  - "Georgia ship live today / US·EU queue for years" says regulatory speed; the script says "what we prove here counts in the West".
  - Headline wraps to two lines.
- **Upgrade:**
  - Headline (one line): **"Georgia: a world hub for first-in-human trials."**
  - Right side: one big line, "**Proven here → counts in the West, years sooner.**" Under it, small: "My hospital is our lab."
  - Map badge: "**25 hospitals · signed**". Either show 25 pins or no count on the map.
  - Move "16–28 months" to Q&A.

### 09 · "Georgia: Land of Validation" TAM/SAM/SOM (script: Ambition)
- **Follows:** Clean size progression (small → big cards) is good hierarchy and flow.
- **Breaks:** The 3-minute script says nothing about market size. Five dollar figures + two multipliers on screen with no voice (rule 13). The title repeats the Georgia slide.
- **Upgrade:** Replace with an **Ambition timeline** that matches your words:
  - **Now** · Georgia · 25-hospital network
  - **9 months** · Lead Georgia + enter Central Asia
  - **Next year** · Raise $5M → take the proof to the U.S.
  
  Three steps, left to right, one arrow. Move TAM/SAM/SOM to the appendix for Q&A.

### 10 · "$200K pre-seed round" (not in the script)
- **Follows:** Simple 2×2, one button, lots of white space.
- **Breaks:** The script says "raise 5 million next year". Showing "$200K now" while saying "$5M next year" confuses the ask.
- **Upgrade (your decision):** Either (a) drop it from the 3-minute demo version and keep it for 1:1 investor meetings, or (b) add one spoken sentence about the $200K to the script. I'd pick (a), because the script is already tight.

### 11 · Close quote
- **Follows:** One line, one real photo, big type, white space. A good emotional end.
- **Breaks:** Wording differs from the script ("one doctor… 1,000 lives" vs "one cardiologist… a few thousand lives"). "Join Us!" is a website button, not a pitch line.
- **Upgrade:** Text: **"As one cardiologist, I can save a few thousand lives. With MediMind, we can save millions."** Swap "Join Us!" for the logo + medimind.md.

## Part 5. Top 7 upgrades, ranked by impact

1. **Make every slide say what you say.** 15 months (not 13); "signed 25-hospital network" (not "live across all 25"); add the second hospital and ~2M encounters; Irakli = CEO, Riot 15 → 500; close quote = "a few thousand lives". When the slide and your voice disagree, investors trust neither.
2. **Rebuild "What We Are" (07) around "One patient walks in. The whole hospital moves."** Names-only cards, light up the same 6 steps you say, in order. Show "within limits each hospital sets". It is the densest slide and your must-keep line.
3. **Replace TAM/SAM/SOM (09) with a 3-step Ambition timeline**: Georgia now → lead Georgia + Central Asia in 9 months → $5M next year → U.S. Take the $200K slide out of the 3-minute version (or add one spoken line for it).
4. **Case slide (04): tell only the miss, then hit "1 in 18".** Remove the "With MediMind" row (save the payoff for 07). Add a giant "1 in 18" + "AHRQ 2022". Relabel to "from my practice". Fix the empty box.
5. **Competition (06): put MediMind on the slide.** Three grey cards (AI tools · AI add-ons · old record systems) with ✗, arrow to one bright MediMind card with ✓ "runs the hospital proactively". Keep "category-defining company".
6. **Team (05): two big founders, one proof each; advisors small.** Same photo size. Fix the broken Healthycore chip icon.
7. **Cut slide 03 and add rhythm.** Drop "doctors building for doctors" (no words for it; reuse its photo on 04). Give 02 and 07 a full calm-blue background so the deck has light/heavy beats. Validation shows 2 numbers, not 4.
