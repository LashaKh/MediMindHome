## Research notes, not public copy

- This snapshot preserves claims; it does not independently substantiate them.
- The headline expresses ambition but does not name the costly present-day problem.
- We need a clearer reason for the CEO to act and credible proof tied to that reason.
- Three-day delivery and a two-week parallel evaluation are founder-defined offers, not guarantees of full clinical production readiness or hospital cutover.
- Define what unlimited development includes, request prioritization, exclusions, and the treatment of advanced AI costs. Older internal commercial notes and current broad wording are not aligned on this.
- Matched subscription pricing does not establish unchanged total implementation cost or a permanent price lock.
- The proactive hospital AI brain is a direction of travel, not proof of currently autonomous clinical operation.
