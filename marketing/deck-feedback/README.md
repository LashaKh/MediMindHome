## Claim discipline

- Contracted hospitals, sites live, capabilities live, migrated records and registered studies are different quantities.
- The earlier lab rate is a documented minimum for a defined cohort, not an exhaustive clinical error rate or proof of savings.
- Irakli's KLAS, Marburger Bund, PLOS and Supreme Court figures are source leads until the exact primary sources and wording have been checked. No figure in his supplied outline was certified by this archive.
- FHIR R4 describes the data standard. Data rights, export access, source rights and continuity also need concrete contract and delivery terms; the standard alone does not prove “never locked in”.
- Existing AI tools, tools under evaluation and a future hospital AI Brain must have different availability labels. Do not turn “after 90 days” into a promise that the Brain ships on day 91.
- Say “operating system”; retain “built ground-up on FHIR R4” when making a first-claim; never claim “the first”.
