## Reframes

> [!tip] Pitch as a story, opening with the case study ^insight-storytelling-pitch
> Nicholas [00:16:13]: the slides are *"very technical very dry very numbers not a lot of visuals"*. His order [00:16:49]: (1) open with GeoHospitals, logo *"has to be massive"*, and a before/after table (time, money, throughput); (2) the problem, *"Don't be overly technical"*, then stop and ask whether it resonates and what other problems they have; (3) MediMind, where *"our co-founder works in the industry so it's not Created by random software guys"*, solving their problems one by one, module by module; (4) the 90-day success plan as the "what's next". The case study is *"your most important assets"* [00:21:53]: it creates FOMO and credibility.

> [!tip] The 90-day success plan sells the roadmap, not the features ^insight-90-day-success-plan
> Nicholas [00:16:49]: a roadmap split into 30, 60 and 90 days, five to seven bullets each, saying what will be delivered and what success looks like (*"probably say success criteria instead of focus"*). *"don't talk about features talk about ... what you're gonna do for them"*. Asked whether this is standard in B2B, he said *"I don't think so"* and that it helped another company close a deal (name garbled, "Singh Monkey") [00:21:53]. It also creates urgency: the dated milestones only hold if they decide by a given date [00:21:53].

> [!tip] The demo belongs in onboarding, not in the sale ^insight-demo-in-onboarding
> Irakli [00:24:47]: prospects ask to see the system, go into patient-flow details, and *"that leads to... to nowhere usually"*; the UI is *"nice and neat but it's not mind-blowing"*, the value is the unified system [00:27:03]. Nicholas [00:25:46]: *"my style is that we'll close the deal without demoing"*, because an on-the-spot demo raises objections. Tell them the demo happens in onboarding (roughly days 0-7 of the 90-day plan) after signing; if they push, ask what stops them working together given unlimited features and the second largest hospital chain already using MediMind. He added *"I don't know what's the outcome"* and suggested also asking [[Brianna Salinas|Brianna]], the sales mentor, the same question [00:27:41].
