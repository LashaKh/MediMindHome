# Irakli - Proposed Hospital Deck Outline

The text below is the supplied feedback, preserved as reference. It is a proposed narrative, not a new contract, verified statistics register, or proof that every capability is live.

## Supplied text

1. Geohospital is replacing full stack, today we are here, etc etc.

2. The reason they are replacing is two fold:
   1. We are solving all current problems with hospital software
   2. We are enabling AI native hospitals of the future

3. Let's start with current problems. Most current software:

   - Takes doctors and nurses away from patients, and pushes them out.
     Nurses losing 3+ hours a week to charting: 46% burned out, 34% likely to leave (KLAS, 2025, 80,147 nurses).
     German hospital doctors lose ~3 hours a day to unnecessary bureaucracy; 28% are considering leaving patient care (Marburger Bund, 2024, 9,649 doctors).

   - Doesn't share a common standard.
     86% of Georgian hospital beds are private, each hospital running its own system with inconsistent standards; the national record is "frequently incomplete" and "seldom used by clinicians" (PLOS Digital Health, 2026).

   - Turns small documentation gaps into payment fights.
     A 455 GEL claim refused because the ministry portal and Form 100 described the same treatment differently. The hospital won, 6 years later (Supreme Court of Georgia, 2026).

   - Doesn't show management where the money is made and lost.
     (Irakli, own voice)

   - [Only if Lasha confirms] Makes staff document twice, on paper and in the system.

   Stop and ask: "Is this what you see too? What else slows your team down?"

4. We are solving that with the team that is capable (highlight Lasha being doctor, Irakli as tech AI leader and Singapore/US/Hong Kong 10 years).

5. What changes for them. Write benefits, not features. "Every system your staff uses, one place, one login" instead of "unified architecture".
For FHIR R4: built on the international standard, so the data is theirs and they're never locked to a vendor.
Feature requests: an answer within three workdays and the feature implemented a week after that.

6. If you agree today this will happen in the next 90 days. Split it into days 0-30, 31-60 and 61-90, with 5-7 bullets each saying what gets delivered and the success criteria. Onboarding and the system walkthrough happen in days 0-7. This is where the demo lives. Close it with: "If you decide by [date], here's what you'd have by [date]."

7. After 90 days: AI native hospital (phase 2 and 3). What running on MediMind makes possible next, 2-3 concrete examples of what already exists. Your hospital decides when.

8. Offer: Free implementation, unlimited feature requests, and ownership of the source code for the initial version if MediMind doesn't deliver.
The limited offer for the next five hospitals.
