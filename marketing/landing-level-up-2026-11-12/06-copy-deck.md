## How to read this deck

- **Text levels.** Every section has exactly three levels:
  - **[L1]** the section headline (Inter 800). Big stat figures are display numbers styled at L1 size.
  - **[L2]** the one support line (Inter 400, larger body).
  - **[L3]** everything small: eyebrow, labels, card titles (600), body lines, captions, footnotes (400).
- **Accent phrase.** Words inside `{curly braces}` are the one accent-blue phrase in a headline. Do not print the braces.
- **Flags.** `showCustomerName` (default `false`) and `showFoundingPlaces` (default `true`). Where a flag changes wording, both versions are given side by side. A third flag is proposed in Part 8 (`showPracticeCase`).
- **Copy is in plain lines after the label.** No quotation marks unless they are part of the copy.
- **Assumptions I acted on (one line each):**
  1. Present tense is used for what the operating system does, because it is built and running beside the current system (master brief: "must read as a complete product today"). "In daily use" is said only for laboratory and imaging at one hospital. The proactive-hospital vision uses "will" throughout and sits under an "In development" label.
  2. The 30-minute call is the "scoping session" of the v6 deck (`04` §8): the reader leaves it with a 90-day plan; the product walkthrough happens in week one of that plan, on their data, not on the call.
- **Section anchor ids** (for nav and scroll): `#top`, `#proof`, `#problem`, `#solution`, `#examples`, `#plan`, `#ai`, `#vision`, `#safety`, `#team`, `#offer`, `#faq`, `#contact`.
