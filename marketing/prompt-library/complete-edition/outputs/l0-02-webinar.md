## Section 7 — What you will learn

- **Draw** a four-column map of one patient journey: action, responsible team, information needed, and where the handoff stalls.
- **Spot** repeated entry or reconciliation that looks small in each department but grows along the whole path.
- **Choose** a baseline measure, such as entries repeated per case or minutes from one handoff to the next, that your team can observe without claiming a patient outcome.
- **Set** a narrow evaluation boundary: the case type, owners, readiness checks, and evidence needed before considering a production change.
