**Automatic colour logic for Google Sheets.** Keep this visible six-column report in A:F. In hidden G put `Eligible?` as `1` only when the stage is live, measurement is verified, and at least one relevant organization is eligible for the week; otherwise `0`. In hidden H put the numerical gate for that row: TOFU `1`, MOFU `1` when eligible, BOFU `1` only if a commercial decision is due, AFU `1` as a 100% fraction of due activations (or the specific agreed milestone), LOFU `1` only when a permissioned referral/review ask is due. Put numeric values in C and D (AFU as a fraction, e.g. `1/2 = 0.5`) and enter N/A only through G. In F2, copy down:

```gs
=IF(G2=0,"N/A",IF(C2="","N/A",IF(C2>=H2,"Green",IF(C2>0,"Amber","Red"))))
```

For a due AFU cohort with 0/2 completed, this is red; 1/2 amber; 2/2 green. BOFU and LOFU do **not** become red just because a long sales cycle has no payment/referral this week: they are N/A until a real due decision/eligible customer exists. Use conditional formatting on F2:F6 with text equal to `Green`, `Amber`, or `Red` for the three colours; keep `N/A` grey. If data has not been reconciled, leave C blank and the formula returns N/A. This avoids a fake 0% when the denominator is zero, but an eligible, due opportunity with zero progress is correctly red. **Provisional:** if a rate has fewer than 20 eligible organizations or a page rate fewer than 100 relevant sessions, append “small sample” in the underlying notes and do not infer a market benchmark from colour alone. The 90-day demo pace is shown in Part 4 and discussed in the Monday decision even though the library's five-stage table uses paid conversion for BOFU.

## Part 4 — North star dashboard

The north star is the **cumulative number of distinct hospital buying organizations that have held a qualified demo**, not booked slots or repeat calls. Calculation: the 90-day scenario asks for 12 in 13 weeks. With the first two weeks used to fix capture and begin outreach, the average required across the next 11 weeks is `12 ÷ 11 = 1.09` held demos/week. The deliberately back-loaded plan below is **harder** than that average: 2 by week 4, 4 by week 8, then 8 more in weeks 9–13 (`8 ÷ 5 = 1.6`/week). To make that plausible as a planning exercise, Prompt 6 models 60 distinct researched organizations → 30 substantive exchanges → 18 accepted bookings → 12 held qualified demos, then 3 readiness-approved evaluations and 1 new paying organization as a stretch. None of those rates is observed. If early volume does not support the last-five-week pace, revise the plan openly; do not relabel bookings as held demos.
