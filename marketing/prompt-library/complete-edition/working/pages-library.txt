HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT

This document contains 3 action-based prompts to help you build publish-ready conversion
pages from scratch. Each prompt is self-contained — use them independently in any order
depending on which page you are building first.


IMPORTANT: Each prompt is designed to be copy-pasted directly into Claude, ChatGPT, or
any AI. Fill in every field marked in brackets before running the prompt. The AI will write
every section of your page — do not run the prompt with empty fields. The more specific
your inputs, the stronger the output.

NOW BUILD THE PAGE. Output every section in order with the exact copy written.
Follow this structure precisely:


SECTION 1 — HEADLINE
Write 3 options. Each must lead with the customer's outcome, be under 10 words, and name
the pain or gain explicitly. Mark your recommended option with [RECOMMENDED].


SECTION 2 — SUBHEADLINE
1 sentence. Name who this is for, what changes, and how fast.


SECTION 3 — ABOVE-THE-FOLD TRUST LINE
Format: "[number] [customer type] already [outcome]." If no customers yet, write a credibility
line about the founding team.


SECTION 4 — CTA BUTTON TEXT
3 options. Each must include the time commitment. No generic "Book a demo." Mark
recommended.
SECTION 5 — WHAT HAPPENS ON THE DEMO (3 bullets)
3 bullets. Each describes a specific concrete thing the prospect will experience on the call.
Start each with an action verb.


SECTION 6 — PAIN SECTION
3 pain statements in the customer's exact language. Short, punchy, line breaks only. No
bullet points.


SECTION 7 — HOW IT WORKS (3 steps)
Step number + 4-word-max label + 1-sentence explanation. Scannable in under 10 seconds.


SECTION 8 — DIFFERENTIATION SECTION
Max 4 sentences. Acknowledges the competitor, explains precisely why this product is
different. Honest and specific.


SECTION 9 — TESTIMONIAL BLOCK
Intro line + testimonials. Lead with stat callout if one exists. Format: quote → name, title,
company.


SECTION 10 — LOGO BAR CAPTION
1 line above the logo bar. Specific to an outcome — not 'trusted by.'


SECTION 11 — OBJECTION FAQ (4 questions)
Address: (1) sales pressure fear, (2) fit/use case doubt, (3) timeline/effort concern, (4)
pricing curiosity. 2–3 sentences per answer max.


SECTION 12 — PRIVACY LINE
1 sentence under the form. State what you will NOT do with their email.


SECTION 13 — BOTTOM REPEAT CTA
1-sentence restatement of core outcome + CTA button text (same as Section 4
recommended).

SECTION 1 — PAGE HEADLINE

Your job is to write every word and structure every section of a free trial landing page that is
ready to publish today.

Your output must be complete, publish-ready page copy — not suggestions, not a brief, not a
framework. Write the actual copy for every section listed below.

Do not use filler phrases like "powerful," "robust," "seamless," "all-in-one," or
"game-changing." Write like someone who understands exactly what the visitor is afraid of
and exactly what they want to be able to do tomorrow that they cannot do today.

The single job of this page is to get a qualified visitor to start a free trial. Every section exists
to remove a reason not to start — not to impress, not to educate, not to tell the brand story.

NOW BUILD THE PAGE. Output every section in order with the exact copy written.
Follow this structure precisely:


SECTION 1 — HEADLINE
3 options. Each must name the specific outcome, be under 10 words, speak to pain or
transformation. Make the trial feel like the obvious next step. Mark recommended.


SECTION 2 — SUBHEADLINE
1 sentence. Name who this is for, what they'll be able to do, how fast. Reference trial length
and 'no credit card required' if applicable. Max 20 words.


SECTION 3 — PRIMARY CTA BUTTON TEXT
Must not say "Sign Up," "Get Started," or "Try for Free." 3 options. Must communicate what
they are getting. Mark recommended.


SECTION 4 — TRUST STRIP
Format: "[No credit card required] · [Cancel anytime] · [Trial length]". If card IS required,
replace first element with a different reassurance.


SECTION 5 — SOCIAL PROOF BAR
Intro line above logo bar + stat line below it. If no logos: write credibility line and flag
[PLACEHOLDER — add logos when available].


SECTION 6 — THE PROBLEM SECTION
3 pain statements in the customer's exact language. Short punchy lines. Line breaks only.
End with 1 line that signals the trial is the answer — without making it a pitch.


SECTION 7 — WHAT YOU GET IN THE TRIAL (3 feature-to-benefit blocks)
Format: [Short label — 3 words max] / [1-sentence benefit in user's language]. Not what the
feature does — what the user stops doing or is now able to do.
SECTION 8 — 'UP AND RUNNING IN 3 STEPS'
Numbered flow. Each step: 3-word label + 1-sentence description. Step 1 = sign-up. Step 3
= aha moment. Each step must feel achievable in minutes.


SECTION 9 — SOCIAL PROOF BLOCK
Stat callout if exists + testimonials. Format: quote → [name, title, company]. Each quote
must mention a specific result. Intro line: 'Here's what [role] say after their first week.' If
none: [PLACEHOLDER] — do not fabricate.


SECTION 10 — PRODUCT VISUAL CAPTION
1 line below the product screenshot or GIF. Describe what the user is seeing and why it
matters to them. Not 'the dashboard' — what it means for their day.


SECTION 11 — TRUST AND SECURITY BLOCK
3–4 trust signals as short label + 1-sentence explanation. Cover: data security, uptime, what
happens to data at trial end, and one human support signal. Write for the person who has to
approve the tool — not the person who wants to use it.


SECTION 12 — OBJECTION FAQ (4 questions)
Address: (1) billing/credit card fear, (2) setup effort fear, (3) data safety if they don't upgrade,
(4) fit — is this right for my role/company? 2 sentences max per answer.


SECTION 13 — PRICING CONTEXT LINE
Format: "After your trial, plans start from [price]. No surprise fees, no annual lock-in
required." If pricing not public, write the alternative version.


SECTION 14 — BOTTOM REPEAT CTA
1-sentence core outcome restatement + CTA button text (same as Section 3 recommended)
+ trust strip (same as Section 4).
SECTION 15 — PRIVACY LINE
1 sentence under form. State what you will NOT do with their email. No legal language. No
links.




FORMATTING RULES FOR ALL PROMPTS

Label each section clearly. Write actual copy — no placeholders except where you explicitly
left a field blank. If you left a field blank, write [PLACEHOLDER: what goes here] — do not
skip the section. Flag any assumption with: ASSUMPTION: [what you assumed]. Do not
explain your choices. Just write the page. Do not add sections not listed above.
