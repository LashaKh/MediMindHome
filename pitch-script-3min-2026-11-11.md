# MediMind pitch, 3-minute version (2026-11-11)

**[COVER]**
In four years, practicing medicine without an AI will be malpractice. Hello, I'm Lasha, a cardiologist, and with my team, we're building that future today.

**[VALIDATION]**
In 15 months, we went from an idea to a signed contract with a network of 25 hospitals. Last week, a second large hospital agreed to replace all its software with ours. That's around 2 million patient encounters on MediMind.

**[GROWTH / ALL-IN]**
I used to think an AI copilot would fix healthcare. I was wrong. The problem is the whole old system, so we're replacing all of it.

**[CASE → PROBLEM]**
A case from my practice: a patient came into the ER at 2 a.m. with stomach pain after a heart procedure. We were so focused on his heart that we missed his pancreas. The signs were buried in his long history. He spent 20 days in the ICU and barely survived.

This is not rare. In emergency rooms, about 1 in 18 patients gets the wrong diagnosis.

At 2 a.m., people miss things. MediMind doesn't.

**[TEAM]**
My co-founder and CEO, Irakli, spent nine years at Riot Games, helping grow a 15-person studio to 500.

**[COMPETITION]**
Our competitors are AI tools, AI add-ons, and old medical record systems. None of them runs a hospital proactively. MediMind does. We replace all three with one system and an agentic AI harness that does the routine work staff do by hand, making us a category-defining company.

**[WHAT WE ARE]**
We're building an AI that can run the whole hospital and make medical decisions within limits each hospital sets.

Picture that 2 a.m. case with MediMind running. It spots the pancreatitis, orders the blood tests, calls the specialists, arranges the scans, books an ICU bed, and handles the billing.

One patient walks in. The whole hospital moves.

**[GEORGIA + RARE COMBINATION]**
We chose Georgia on purpose. It's a world hub for first-in-human trials, so what we prove here counts in the West, years sooner. My hospital is our lab.

**[AMBITION]**
Within nine months, we plan to lead Georgia and enter Central Asia. Next year, we'll raise 5 million to take that proof to the U.S.

As one cardiologist, I can save a few thousand lives. With MediMind, we can save millions.
